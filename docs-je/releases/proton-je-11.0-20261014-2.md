**Why this release:** one game workaround, for Helldivers 2. Since its update of 2026-10-21 the game closes through its crash reporter with a non-continuable `STATUS_INVALID_HANDLE` raised in `RtlpUnWaitCriticalSection()`, on every Proton: its D3D12 task dispatcher wakes its renderer thread while it holds a critical section in the renderer's stack frame, and the renderer can return and reuse the frame before the dispatcher is out of `LeaveCriticalSection()`. In this build a thread woken from `WaitOnAddress()` by a thread that holds a critical section on the woken thread's stack waits, for at most about 30 ms, until the waker is done with it ([write-up](https://github.com/jegglest/proton-je/blob/proton-je-11.0-20261014-2/docs-je/helldivers2-renderer-race.md)). It is on for `helldivers2.exe` only; `WINE_CS_WAKE_GUARD=0` in the launch options turns it off. Valve's `WINE_ALERT_SIMULATE_SCHED_QUANTUM`, tried first, does nothing on Linux 7.2 and later. Tested with a reproducer and Wine's sync tests; the in-game result is not in yet. Same base and same patches as `-1` otherwise.
